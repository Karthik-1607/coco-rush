# Coco Rush Cinematic Background Credits

The background loop `assam-coco-story.mp4` is a locally optimized composite created for the Coco Rush website from two free-stock clips:

- [Lush Tropical Coconut Palm Plantation](https://www.pexels.com/video/lush-tropical-coconut-palm-plantation-32712335/) — downloaded through Pexels' free video download flow.
- [River, Boats, Island, Majuli, Assam](https://pixabay.com/videos/river-boats-island-majuli-assam-214623/) — downloaded from Pixabay's royalty-free video library.

The source clips were cropped, color-adjusted, muted, and composited into a 1280×720 MP4 loop for web performance. The site uses the resulting composite as an ambient decorative background and does not present it as documentary footage of the product manufacturing process.
